📖 Definitions, filters & SKU decoder

Cohort semantics: Category / Variant / Size / From–To filters apply to each customer's first (entry) order; Depth and Region apply to the whole journey. Metrics, charts and tables all recompute for the selected cohort.

Seasonality tiles: orders counted by the variant's intended season line. Purchase timing: orders by calendar month — Cold Winter Dec–Feb · Hot Dry Mar–May · Hot Humid Jun–Sep · Post-Monsoon Oct–Nov. Strength: Highly ≥40% of orders in the peak timing season · Moderately 30–40% · Evergreen <30%.

Loyalty: V2V % — repeat customers whose 2nd order repeats an entry variant; V2C % — 2nd order stays in an entry category. Flows: Same product = identical SKU · Upsized = larger size · Lateral = same size, different product · Downsized = smaller size.

Geography × seasonality: heat cells show each region's order share per calendar season / intended line — darker means a stronger skew. Affinity index = region's share of a segment ÷ national share (1.00× = exactly average; ❄ winter-line and gel affinities shown). On-season lift = share of a variant's orders inside its intended timing window ÷ that window's baseline share of all orders.

Deep-cut insights: the six strongest auto-detected patterns in the current cohort (regional season concentration, gel affinity, on-season lift champion & mismatch, best-repeating region, entry-season repeat effect, city concentration, downsize pressure). Every finding shows its own numbers and applies its filter on click.

SKU code FC-<LINE>-<VARIANT>-<SIZE> — e.g. FC-HDDM-FB-050 = Face Malai · Hot Dry · Flax Bakuchi · 50g. Lines: CWDM Cold Winter · HDDM Hot Dry · HHDM Hot Humid · DM non-seasonal concern · AC Active Gel · HG Aloe Vera Gel. Multi-item orders count for every item in tiles/loyalty and by their first SKU in transitions.

Loading your file: the picker accepts both the raw order dump (any long-format CSV with customer / order-date / SKU or short-code / city columns — headers are auto-detected) and the journey-wide export. Only moisturiser SKUs (FC-…) are kept; everything else is skipped and counted in the header chip. Journeys are rebuilt per customer: same-day items merge into one order, first 6 kept. Day-first dates and Excel serials are supported.

Privacy: your CSV is read entirely inside your browser — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Self-contained static dashboard · no server, no tracking, no external libraries · works on GitHub Pages
